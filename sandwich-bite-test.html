Sandwich Bite Test

Move your cursor or tap anywhere on the remaining perimeter to choose the next bite. After eight bites, the path is hashed into a custom sandwich gif.

How it works

  1. Every click searches for a valid bite that still touches the exposed sandwich edge.
  2. The bitten area disappears immediately, so new perimeter paths open up after every bite.
  3. After eight bites, the bite coordinates are hashed into a replayable sandwich gif.
