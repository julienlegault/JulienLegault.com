Sandwich Bite Test

Tap the highlighted bite in order to show how you would eat the sandwich. Each red target disappears after the bite animation, and the full-sandwich animation unlocks once the meal is gone.

How it works

  1. Only one bite is live at a time, shown with a bright red target.
  2. Click the active target to play the bite animation and reveal the missing chunk.
  3. Finish all five bites to unlock the full sandwich-eating animation.
